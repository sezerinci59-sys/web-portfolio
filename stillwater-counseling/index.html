What I help with

People come for many reasons. These are the ones I see most often.

  • AnxietyConstant worry, racing thoughts, a body that will not settle.
  • Stress and burnoutRunning on empty, short-tempered, unable to switch off.
  • RelationshipsRepeating arguments, distance, trust, or feeling unheard.
  • Life transitionsA move, a new job, parenthood, separation, or the end of something.
  • GriefLoss of a person, a relationship, or a version of life you expected.

Therapy is a space to understand yourself and feel better supported. It is not a cure, and I will never promise one. I will promise to take you seriously.

What happens in the first session

Not knowing what to expect is often the biggest worry. Here is how it usually goes.

  1. We settle in

    We start with how you are today and how you would like to use our time. You decide how much to share.

  2. You tell me what brought you here

    I ask a few gentle questions about what is going on and what you hope might change. There are no wrong answers.

  3. We agree on a next step

    Before we finish, we talk about how I can help and how often to meet. If I am not the right fit, I will say so and point you elsewhere.

Questions people often ask

Is what I say confidential?

Yes. What you share stays between us. The only exceptions are the legal and ethical limits that apply to all therapists, such as a serious risk to your safety or someone else's. I will explain these clearly in our first conversation.

What if I need to cancel?

Life happens. Please let me know at least 24 hours before your session and we will find another time at no charge. Later cancellations may be charged the full fee.

How do online sessions work?

I send you a private video link before the session. You need a quiet spot, a stable connection, and a phone, tablet or computer. If the link does not work, we can switch to a phone call.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on what you want to work on. Some people feel helped after a handful of sessions, others prefer to continue for longer. We review together regularly, and you can stop at any time.

Can I get a receipt for my insurance?

Yes. I provide an itemised receipt after each session. Whether your insurer reimburses therapy depends on your policy, so it is worth checking with them first.
