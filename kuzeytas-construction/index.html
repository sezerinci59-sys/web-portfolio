What we build

Four kinds of work, each with a clear scope. If your project sits between two of them, tell us and we will price it as it is.

Villas

Detached houses on your plot, from excavation to a finished interior.

Included

  • Structure, roof and insulation
  • Facade, joinery and finishes
  • Landscaping and garden walls

Residential blocks

Small apartment buildings for landowners and family companies.

Included

  • Reinforced concrete frame
  • Mechanical and electrical installation
  • Permit-stage coordination

Industrial buildings

Workshops, warehouses and production halls for small companies.

Included

  • Steel or precast frame
  • Industrial floor slabs
  • Yard, drainage and access roads

Renovation

Strengthening, extending and refitting buildings that already stand.

Included

  • Survey and condition report
  • Structural repair and strengthening
  • Facade, roof and interior works

How we work

The same four steps on every job, so you always know what happens next.

  1. 1

    Site visit and survey

    We walk the plot, check access and ground, and listen to what you need to build.

  2. 2

    Drawings and fixed quote

    You receive drawings and a written fixed price with a schedule before any work starts.

  3. 3

    Construction with weekly photo reports

    Every week you get photos and a short progress note, wherever you are.

  4. 4

    Handover and warranty

    We walk through the finished building with you, fix the snag list and hand over the keys with a written warranty.
